

The Fox and the Goat (Short Story)

A fox fell into a well and could not get out.
A thirsty goat came there to drink water.

The fox said, “This water is very sweet! Jump in and drink.”

The goat jumped in and drank the water. But then he could not get out.

The fox said, “Stand up on your back legs. I will climb and get out. Then I will help you.”

The goat helped the fox. The fox jumped out… but ran away!

The fox said, “You should think before you act!”

Moral: Think before you do anything
