The Crow and the Pitcher

On a hot day, a thirsty crow was flying in search of water.
After a long time, it found a pitcher with a little water at the bottom.

The crow tried to drink, but its beak could not reach the water.
It thought for a while and got an idea.

The crow picked up small stones one by one and dropped them into the pitcher.
Slowly, the water level rose up.

At last, the crow was able to drink the water and flew away happily.

Moral:

Where there is a will, there is a way.
