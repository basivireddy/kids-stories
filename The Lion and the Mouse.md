The Lion and the Mouse

One day, a lion was sleeping in the forest.
A little mouse ran over his body and woke him up.

The lion got angry and caught the mouse.
“Why did you wake me up? I will eat you!” he roared.

The mouse was very scared and said,
“Please forgive me, O King! One day I will help you.”

The lion laughed.
“You are so small! How can you help me?”
But he let the mouse go.

A few days later, the lion was caught in a hunter’s net.
He roared loudly for help.

The mouse heard the roar and came quickly.
It started biting the net with its sharp teeth.

Soon, the net broke, and the lion was free.

The lion thanked the mouse and said,
“You are small, but you helped me a lot!”

Moral:

No act of kindness is ever wasted.
